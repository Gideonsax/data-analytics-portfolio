### 2. Health Data Quality and Ward Matching Analysis

**Tools:** Python, pandas, RapidFuzz, Excel, openpyxl

**Focus:** Data management, geographic data harmonisation, health information systems

Developed a data-matching workflow to reconcile ward records across health and administrative datasets. The project addresses inconsistencies in state, local government area (LGA), and ward names that can affect population analysis and programme reporting.

**Key features**
- Exact matching of standardised administrative names.
- Fuzzy matching for spelling variations and inconsistent labels.
- Similarity scoring to support review of uncertain matches.
- Classification of records into matched, unmatched, and review categories.
- Preservation of original values alongside proposed corrections.
- Export of validation results for further review in Excel.

**Business value:** Supports more consistent geographic identifiers, improves data integration, and reduces the burden of manual record reconciliation.

**Project folder:** `projects/health-data-quality/`

**Status:** Prototype developed; documentation and portfolio-ready outputs in progress.

## How I Approach Data Projects

Each completed case study will document:

1. **Problem definition:** The operational or analytical question.
2. **Data preparation:** Cleaning, validation, transformation, and quality checks.
3. **Methodology:** The tools, metrics, and analytical methods used.
4. **Findings:** The results supported by the available data.
5. **Recommendations:** Practical actions informed by the findings.
6. **Limitations:** Data constraints, assumptions, and areas requiring further investigation.
